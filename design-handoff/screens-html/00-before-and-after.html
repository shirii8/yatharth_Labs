Your health file, kept by people who know you.

One idea, used everywhere. Each report is a page in your file, behind its own tab. A person at the lab files it, writes on it and calls you when it matters. Anything that is not part of a file, a page, a tab or a person was cut.

The motif: the index tab, recognisable from any crop

BeforeAfter, and why
Idea A letter from the lab: right mood, but the envelope did nothing after the list page. Your health file. Tabs, pages and notes carry the whole journey, from sign-in to the front desk.
Motif Drawings and rules appeared here and there with no shared shape. The index tab everywhere: languages, family, results, your marker on the ruler, dividers, empty states.
Accent Black buttons; olive and terracotta both used for status. Ink-green #0F5A43 means only "yours" or "the next step". Status words stay muted and always carry a word.
Scale Every heading 30 to 36 px; pages felt even. One serif moment per screen at 52 to 96 px (a name, a number, a sentence), then quiet 15 to 19 px text.
Rhythm Report sections spaced the same top to bottom. The report breathes at the top (greeting, one result) and tightens below (steps, copies, ledger).
Frame Soft cards with thin borders. A heavy double-ruled page on a warm desk, with tinted tabs, borrowed from good printed folders.
States Correct but generic copy. A kettle, an empty folder, a door ajar, an unlit lamp, a filed-away page, signed by the front desk in a human voice.
Compact The Simple page, smaller. Sharper: tighter leading, square corners, a trend line with one sentence. Same file, grown up.
